description: >-
  Lee Martineau is the Co Founder and CEO of Terra Preta Organics, a Canadian
  manufacturing company focused on restoring soil function in degraded
  landscapes. Based in Alberta, Lee leads the development of regenerative soil
  amendment pellets designed specifically for energy sector reclamation,
  ecological restoration, and large scale land recovery.


  Lee did not come from a traditional soil science background. His work began with a practical question: how do we rebuild soil in a way that actually restores biological activity, nutrient cycling, structure, and long term function rather than simply meeting short term compliance targets? That question led to years of formulation, field testing, and collaboration with industry partners, regulators, and landowners.


  Under his leadership, Terra Preta Organics has developed pelletized blends that combine organic nutrition, mineral balancing, and biological activation into a format that is practical at scale. The company’s focus is not just on adding nutrients, but on rebuilding living soil systems that support resilient plant communities and durable reclamation outcomes.


  Lee works closely with environmental consultants, oil and gas operators, and restoration professionals to design site specific solutions that align with regulatory requirements while improving soil performance. His approach integrates applied soil science, manufacturing innovation, and data driven formulation.


  He lives in rural Alberta with his family, where he also farms and grows garlic. His long term vision is to help shift how we define successful reclamation by moving beyond surface metrics and toward measurable soil function.
